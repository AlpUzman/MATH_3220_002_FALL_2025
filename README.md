# MATH_3220_002_FALL_2025

This is MATH 3220-002, the advanced multivariable calculus class at the University of Utah. 

---

Much like its prequel MATH 3210, MATH 3220 is a proof based class, and the emphasis will be on developing "mathematical maturity", in particular on rigorous argumentation, rather than calculation of the values of certain limits, integrals or derivatives. We'll start with a review of linear algebra, and an introduction to metric spaces. Then we'll discuss differential and integral calculus of multivariable vector valued functions. Finally we'll bring together differential and integral calculi through the formalism of differential forms.
